04
MedRx
Fine-Tuned Clinical Drug & Treatment Recommendation LLM

MedRx is a fine-tuned clinical LLM built on Microsoft Phi-4-mini-Instruct (3.8B parameters), trained using QLoRA (4-bit NF4 quantization) with Unsloth on a LoRA adapter over 13,000+ clinical samples curated from 4 public medical sources. A multi-stage data pipeline handles PHI scrubbing, 7-gate quality filtering, MD5-based deduplication, and drug-class-templated synthetic augmentation for USMLE-style clinical reasoning cases. A post-inference safety layer performs drug-allergen cross-reactivity checks and dosage-range validation against a structured Pydantic-parsed output schema. The build also involved diagnosing GPU architecture-specific training failures — Triton JIT compiler segmentation faults on Ada Lovelace hardware — through root-cause analysis of native stack traces, with the final model published publicly on Hugging Face.

05
PrepAI
AI-Powered Interview Simulation Platform

PrepAI is an AI-powered interview simulator that generates context-aware questions drawn from job descriptions and company-specific expectations, with adaptive follow-up questioning that reacts to how a candidate answers. It evaluates performance across five dimensions — technical accuracy, communication, business impact, confidence, and structured thinking — and produces detailed performance reports with STAR-method recommendations and candidate scoring out of 10. Built on a scalable full-stack architecture using Next.js and Node.js/Express, with Groq-powered Llama 3.3 70B inference driving the simulation engine.
