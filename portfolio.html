04
ITCPulse
AI-Powered GST ITC Recovery Engine

ITCPulse is an AI-powered financial intelligence system designed to detect and recover unclaimed GST Input Tax Credit (ITC) for Indian businesses. By analyzing GSTR-2B data and purchase registers, it identifies mismatches, non-filing suppliers, and time-sensitive credit losses. The system provides actionable insights such as supplier follow-up lists, discrepancy detection, and monthly recovery reports — enabling finance teams to recover lakhs of rupees in lost working capital. Built as a local-first SaaS intelligence layer, it transforms manual reconciliation into an automated, high-impact decision system.

05
Automated Reconciliation Agent In Progress
AI-Powered Financial Close Automation

An AI-driven reconciliation system designed to automate the month-end financial close process at scale. Inspired by real-world challenges in high-volume payment systems like Stripe, the agent matches transaction data with General Ledger entries, identifies discrepancies, and categorizes them into actionable insights such as timing differences, currency mismatches, and missing records. It generates human-readable summary reports and prioritized exception lists, significantly reducing manual effort and accelerating financial close cycles for finance teams.
